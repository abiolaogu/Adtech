# Video Training Script: Advertiser Onboarding
**Duration**: 15 minutes | **Target**: New advertisers

---

## Scene 1: Welcome & Platform Overview (2 min)

**[SCREEN: Platform homepage]**

**Narrator**: "Welcome to the AdTech Platform! I'm Sarah, and I'll guide you through creating your first successful campaign. By the end of this tutorial, you'll know how to set up campaigns, upload creatives, and track performance."

**[SCREEN: Dashboard overview with animated elements]**

**Narrator**: "This is your dashboard - your command center for all advertising activities. You'll see real-time performance metrics, active campaigns, and quick actions."

**[HIGHLIGHT: Key metrics cards]**

**Narrator**: "These cards show impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend - your most important metrics at a glance."

---

## Scene 2: Creating Your First Campaign (5 min)

**[SCREEN: Click "New Campaign" button]**

**Narrator**: "Let's create your first campaign. Click the '+ New Campaign' button."

**[SCREEN: Campaign creation form]**

**Narrator**: "Step 1: Campaign Details. Give your campaign a descriptive name like 'Summer Sale 2025' so you can easily identify it later."

**[TYPE ANIMATION: "Summer Sale 2025"]**

**Narrator**: "Next, select your objective. Are you looking for brand awareness, website traffic, or conversions? For e-commerce, choose 'Conversions'."

**[CLICK: "Conversions" radio button]**

**Narrator**: "Set your budget. I recommend starting with $50 per day for testing, then scaling up based on results."

**[TYPE: "$50" in daily budget field]**

**Narrator**: "Step 2: Targeting. This is where the magic happens! Let's target US users aged 25-45 interested in electronics."

**[SCREEN: Geographic targeting]**
**[SELECT: United States]**

**Narrator**: "Select United States..."

**[SCREEN: Demographics]**
**[DRAG: Age slider to 25-45]**

**Narrator**: "...set age range to 25-45..."

**[SCREEN: Interests]**
**[TYPE: "Electronics"]**
**[SELECT: Multiple interest categories]**

**Narrator**: "...and add interest targeting. Our AI will find users who've shown interest in electronics, recently visited tech websites, and are likely to convert."

---

## Scene 3: Uploading Creatives (3 min)

**[SCREEN: Creative upload interface]**

**Narrator**: "Now let's add your ads. Click 'Upload Creative'."

**[SCREEN: Drag and drop interface]**

**Narrator**: "Simply drag and drop your image files. We support all standard sizes: 300x250, 728x90, and more."

**[ANIMATION: Files being dragged onto screen]**

**Narrator**: "Add your landing page URL - this is where users go when they click your ad."

**[TYPE: "https://yourstore.com/summer-sale"]**

**Narrator**: "Pro tip: Use a dedicated landing page that matches your ad message for better conversion rates."

**[SCREEN: Creative preview]**

**Narrator**: "Preview how your ad looks on desktop and mobile before submitting for approval. Our team reviews all creatives within 24 hours to ensure quality."

---

## Scene 4: Analytics & Optimization (3 min)

**[SCREEN: Campaign analytics dashboard with graphs]**

**Narrator**: "After your campaign launches, visit the Analytics tab to track performance."

**[HIGHLIGHT: Performance graphs]**

**Narrator**: "These graphs show impressions, clicks, and conversions over time. Look for trends - are certain days performing better?"

**[SCREEN: Top performing creatives report]**

**Narrator**: "The Creative Performance report shows which ads are working best. See that creative with 3.2% CTR? That's a winner! Pause underperformers and create more winners like this."

**[ANIMATION: Pause button click on low-performing creative]**

**Narrator**: "Don't be afraid to pause low performers. This increases your overall campaign ROI."

**[SCREEN: Geographic performance heatmap]**

**Narrator**: "Check which locations are converting best. If California is crushing it but Florida isn't, adjust your bids accordingly."

---

## Scene 5: Best Practices & Next Steps (2 min)

**[SCREEN: Best practices checklist with checkmarks]**

**Narrator**: "Here are the top 5 tips for campaign success:"

**[CHECKMARKS APPEAR ONE BY ONE]**

✅ "1. Start with automatic bidding - let our AI optimize"
✅ "2. Test multiple creatives - at least 3 per campaign"
✅ "3. Give campaigns 7 days to learn before making big changes"
✅ "4. Monitor daily for the first week, then weekly"
✅ "5. Scale winners, pause losers"

**[SCREEN: Resource links]**

**Narrator**: "Need more help? Check out our comprehensive user manual, join live webinars every Tuesday, or contact support 24/7 via chat."

**[SCREEN: Call to action]**

**Narrator**: "You're ready to launch your first campaign! Click the 'Launch Campaign' button and watch your business grow. Happy advertising!"

**[FADE TO: Platform logo]**

## Production Notes

**Visual Elements**:
- Screen recordings from actual platform
- Animated arrows/highlights for key elements
- Split-screen for desktop/mobile previews
- Performance graphs with real data
- Smooth transitions between sections

**Audio**:
- Professional voiceover (female, friendly, confident)
- Background music (subtle, non-distracting)
- Sound effects for button clicks, transitions

**Captions**:
- Full closed captions for accessibility
- Key metrics highlighted in text overlays
- Action items in bold text

**Interactive Elements** (for HTML5 video player):
- Clickable chapters for easy navigation
- Pause points with quick tips
- Quiz at the end (optional)
- "Try it yourself" CTAs linking to platform
