## Campaign Management

### Creating a New Campaign

#### Step 1: Campaign Details

1. **Click** "+ New Campaign" button
2. **Enter** campaign name (descriptive, e.g., "Holiday Sale - December 2025")
3. **Select** campaign objective:
   - 🎯 **Brand Awareness** - Maximize impressions
   - 🖱️ **Traffic** - Drive clicks to website
   - 🛒 **Conversions** - Generate sales/leads
   - 📱 **App Installs** - Mobile app downloads
   - 📹 **Video Views** - Video engagement

4. **Set** campaign dates:
   - Start date (immediate or scheduled)
   - End date (optional, or continuous)

**Example**:
```
Campaign Name: "Black Friday Electronics Sale"
Objective: Conversions
Start Date: Nov 20, 2025, 12:00 AM
End Date: Nov 30, 2025, 11:59 PM
```

#### Step 2: Budget & Bidding

**Budget Options**:

1. **Daily Budget**
   - Amount spent per day
   - Campaign paces evenly throughout the day
   - Example: $100/day = ~$4.17/hour

2. **Total Budget**
   - Lifetime campaign budget
   - System optimizes spend across campaign duration
   - Example: $3,000 total

**Bidding Strategies**:

| Strategy | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Automatic** | AI optimizes bids for best results | Beginners, most campaigns |
| **Manual CPC** | Set cost-per-click bid | Experienced advertisers |
| **Target CPA** | Set target cost-per-acquisition | Performance campaigns |
| **Maximize Clicks** | Get most clicks within budget | Traffic campaigns |

**💡 Recommendation**: Start with Automatic bidding, then optimize based on performance.

#### Step 3: Targeting

##### Geographic Targeting

**Country Level**:
- Select one or multiple countries
- Example: United States, Canada, United Kingdom

**Region/State Level**:
- Drill down to specific states/provinces
- Example: California, New York, Texas

**City Level**:
- Target specific cities
- Radius targeting (e.g., 10 miles around downtown)

**DMA (Designated Market Area)**:
- TV market regions
- Example: New York DMA, Los Angeles DMA

##### Demographic Targeting

**Age Range**:
- 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+
- Multiple selections allowed

**Gender**:
- Male, Female, All

**Income Level**:
- <$25k, $25k-$50k, $50k-$75k, $75k-$100k, $100k+

**Education**:
- High School, Some College, Bachelor's, Graduate Degree

##### Interest Targeting

Browse or search from 500+ interest categories:

**Top Categories**:
- 🛍️ Shopping & Fashion
- 🏠 Home & Garden
- 🚗 Automotive
- 💼 Business & Finance
- ⚽ Sports & Fitness
- 🎮 Gaming
- 🍔 Food & Dining
- ✈️ Travel

**Custom Audiences**:
- Upload customer email lists (CRM integration)
- Website visitors (pixel-based retargeting)
- App users
- Lookalike audiences (AI-generated similar users)

##### Device Targeting

**Device Type**:
- 📱 Mobile
- 💻 Desktop
- 📺 Connected TV (CTV)
- 🖥️ Tablet

**Operating System**:
- iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux

**Browser**:
- Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge

#### Step 4: Ad Placement

**Automatic Placement** (Recommended):
- AI places ads across optimal inventory
- Maximizes reach and performance

**Manual Placement**:
- Choose specific sites/apps
- Set bids per placement
- Useful for brand safety

**Placement Categories**:
- 📰 News & Media
- 🎮 Gaming Apps
- 📱 Social Media
- 🎬 Video Content
- 📧 Email Newsletters

**Exclusions**:
- Block specific domains
- Content categories to avoid
- Example: Exclude adult content, gambling sites

#### Step 5: Review & Launch

**Campaign Summary**:
```
✓ Campaign Name: Black Friday Electronics Sale
✓ Objective: Conversions
✓ Budget: $100/day ($3,000 total)
✓ Duration: Nov 20 - Nov 30 (11 days)
✓ Targeting: US, Ages 25-54, Electronics shoppers
✓ Devices: Desktop & Mobile
✓ Creatives: 3 display ads, 2 video ads
✓ Estimated Reach: 250,000 - 350,000 users
```

**Click** "Launch Campaign" to start!

## Creative Management

### Supported Ad Formats

#### Display Ads

**Standard Sizes** (IAB compliant):
- **300x250** - Medium Rectangle (Most popular)
- **728x90** - Leaderboard
- **160x600** - Wide Skyscraper
- **320x50** - Mobile Banner
- **300x600** - Half Page
- **970x250** - Billboard

**Specifications**:
- File types: JPG, PNG, GIF (animated allowed)
- Max file size: 150 KB
- Resolution: 72 DPI minimum
- Color mode: RGB

#### Video Ads

**Formats**:
- **In-Stream** - Pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll
- **Out-Stream** - Standalone video player
- **Native Video** - In-feed video

**Specifications**:
- File types: MP4, MOV, AVI
- Max file size: 100 MB
- Duration: 6s, 15s, 30s (recommended: 15s)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1280x720 (HD)
- Frame rate: 24-30 fps
- Bitrate: 5-10 Mbps

#### Native Ads

**Components**:
- Headline (max 25 characters)
- Description (max 90 characters)
- Image (1200x628 recommended)
- Call-to-action button
- Brand logo (300x300)

### Uploading Creatives

**Step-by-Step**:

1. **Navigate** to Creatives → Upload New
2. **Select** ad format (Display, Video, Native)
3. **Upload** files:
   - Drag & drop files
   - Or click "Browse" to select
4. **Enter** creative details:
   - Creative name (internal reference)
   - Landing page URL
   - Call-to-action text
5. **Add** tracking:
   - Impression tracking pixel (optional)
   - Click tracking parameters
6. **Submit** for review

**Review Process**:
- Automated scan: <5 minutes
- Manual review (if needed): Up to 24 hours
- You'll receive email notification

**Creative Policies**:
- ✅ Allowed: Product images, logos, promotional text
- ❌ Prohibited: Adult content, weapons, tobacco, fake news, misleading claims

### Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO)

**What is DCO?**
AI automatically tests and optimizes creative elements to find best-performing combinations.

**How to Use**:
1. Upload multiple versions of:
   - Headlines (3-5 variants)
   - Images (3-5 variants)
   - Descriptions (2-3 variants)
   - CTAs (2-3 variants)
2. Enable "Dynamic Creative Optimization"
3. AI tests combinations and serves top performers

**Example**:
```
Headlines:
- "Save 50% on Electronics"
- "Black Friday Sale - Electronics"
- "Massive Electronics Discounts"

Images:
- [Laptop image]
- [TV image]
- [Phone image]

= 9 ad variations automatically generated and tested
```

## Budget & Bidding

### Budget Management

#### Setting Budgets

**Daily Budget**:
- Minimum: $10/day
- Recommended: $50-$100/day for optimal performance
- System paces spend evenly throughout 24 hours

**Total Budget**:
- Minimum: $100 lifetime
- No maximum
- Campaign runs until budget exhausted

**Budget Scheduling**:
- Set different budgets for different days
- Example: $200 on weekends, $100 on weekdays
- Useful for promotions or events

#### Budget Alerts

Configure alerts:
- 50% budget spent
- 75% budget spent
- 90% budget spent
- Daily budget exceeded

**Notification methods**:
- Email
- SMS (premium feature)
- In-app notification
- Mobile app push notification

### Bidding Strategies

#### Automatic Bidding (Recommended for Beginners)

**How it works**:
- AI analyzes millions of data points
- Automatically adjusts bids in real-time
- Optimizes for campaign objective
- No manual bid management needed

**Best for**:
- New advertisers
- Standard campaigns
- When you trust the AI

**Expected results**:
- 20-30% better performance vs manual
- Learns and improves over 7-14 days

#### Manual CPC (Cost-Per-Click)

**How it works**:
- You set maximum CPC bid
- Example: $0.50 CPC = max $0.50 per click
- Actual cost usually lower (second-price auction)

**When to use**:
- You know optimal CPC for your business
- Tight budget control needed
- Testing specific bid amounts

**Tips**:
- Start with platform's suggested bid
- Adjust based on performance
- Higher bids = more traffic, but watch ROI

#### Target CPA (Cost-Per-Acquisition)

**How it works**:
- Set target cost per conversion
- Example: $20 CPA = willing to pay $20 per sale
- AI optimizes bids to achieve target

**Requirements**:
- Conversion tracking must be enabled
- Minimum 50 conversions in last 30 days
- Consistent conversion volume

**Best for**:
- E-commerce
- Lead generation
- Clear conversion value

#### Maximize Clicks

**How it works**:
- Gets maximum clicks within budget
- AI bids to maximize click volume
- Ignores conversion quality

**Best for**:
- Traffic campaigns
- Brand awareness
- Building retargeting audience

### Bid Adjustments

Fine-tune bids by:

**Device**:
- +50% for mobile (if mobile converts better)
- -30% for desktop
- +20% for tablets

**Time of Day**:
- +100% during 6pm-10pm (peak conversion times)
- -50% during 12am-6am (low conversion times)

**Day of Week**:
- +25% on weekends
- -20% on Mondays

**Geography**:
- +50% for high-value cities (NYC, SF, LA)
- -30% for low-performing regions

**Audience**:
- +100% for website visitors (retargeting)
- +50% for lookalike audiences
- 0% for cold audiences (baseline)

**Example**:
```
Base bid: $1.00
Device: +50% (mobile)
Time: +100% (8pm)
Audience: +100% (retargeting)
= Actual bid: $1.00 × 1.5 × 2.0 × 2.0 = $6.00
```

## Analytics & Reporting

### Performance Metrics

#### Campaign Metrics

**Reach & Frequency**:
- **Impressions**: Total ad views
- **Reach**: Unique users who saw ad
- **Frequency**: Average times user saw ad
  - Optimal: 3-7 times
  - Too high (>15): Ad fatigue

**Engagement**:
- **Clicks**: Total clicks on ad
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100
  - Display avg: 0.5-1%
  - Good: >1.5%
  - Excellent: >3%
- **CPC (Cost-Per-Click)**: Total spend ÷ Clicks
  - Display avg: $0.50-$2.00
  - Video avg: $0.10-$0.30

**Conversions**:
- **Conversions**: Completed desired actions
- **CVR (Conversion Rate)**: Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100
  - E-commerce avg: 2-3%
  - Lead gen avg: 5-10%
- **CPA (Cost-Per-Acquisition)**: Total spend ÷ Conversions
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**: Revenue ÷ Spend × 100
  - Break-even: 100%
  - Profitable: >150%
  - Excellent: >300%

## Best Practices

### Campaign Optimization

#### Week 1: Learning Phase
- ✅ Let AI gather data (don't make changes)
- ✅ Monitor for major issues only
- ❌ Don't panic about low initial performance
- ❌ Don't adjust bids/budgets

#### Week 2: Initial Optimization
- Review performance data
- Pause low-performing creatives (CTR <0.3%)
- Adjust targeting (exclude poor audiences)
- Test bid adjustments

#### Week 3+: Continuous Optimization
- A/B test new creatives weekly
- Expand to new audiences
- Adjust budgets based on ROAS
- Scale winners, pause losers

### Creative Best Practices

**Display Ads**:
- ✅ Clear, simple message
- ✅ Strong call-to-action
- ✅ High-quality images
- ✅ Consistent branding
- ❌ Too much text
- ❌ Blurry images
- ❌ Generic stock photos

**Video Ads**:
- ✅ Hook in first 3 seconds
- ✅ Brand logo visible throughout
- ✅ Captions (80% watch muted)
- ✅ Clear CTA at end
- ❌ Slow intros
- ❌ Audio-dependent content

### Budget Allocation

**80/20 Rule**:
- 80% of budget to proven winners
- 20% of budget to testing new campaigns/audiences

**Minimum budgets for success**:
- Brand awareness: $500/month
- Traffic: $1,000/month
- Conversions: $2,000/month

## Troubleshooting

### Low Impressions

**Possible causes**:
1. Bid too low → Increase bid by 20%
2. Targeting too narrow → Expand audience
3. Budget too small → Increase daily budget
4. Ad not approved → Check approval status

### Low CTR

**Solutions**:
1. Test new creative designs
2. Stronger call-to-action
3. Better ad-audience fit
4. Exclude irrelevant placements

### High CPA

**Solutions**:
1. Improve landing page (load speed, design)
2. Better audience targeting
3. Negative keywords (exclude bad traffic)
4. Optimize conversion funnel
5. Lower bids on poor performers

### Campaign Not Spending

**Checklist**:
- ✓ Campaign is active (not paused)
- ✓ Payment method is valid
- ✓ Daily budget is sufficient (min $10)
- ✓ Targeting is not too narrow
- ✓ Bids are competitive
- ✓ Creatives are approved
