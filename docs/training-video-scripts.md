## 3. Video Scripts

### V01: Platform Overview & Getting Started

**Duration**: 8 minutes
**Audience**: All users
**Objective**: Introduce the platform, explain its value proposition, and guide users through account creation and initial navigation.

**Script Outline:**

[0:00 - 0:30] **Introduction**
- "Welcome to the Adtech Platform training series. In this video, we will walk you through the platform's core capabilities and get you set up with your first account."
- Show platform logo and tagline

[0:30 - 2:00] **Platform Overview**
- "The Adtech Platform is an all-in-one advertising technology solution that combines a Demand-Side Platform for advertisers, a Supply-Side Platform for publishers, a real-time bidding engine, and a Customer Data Platform."
- Screen: Show architecture diagram
- "Key capabilities include real-time bidding with AI optimization, multi-format ad serving, audience segmentation, fraud detection, and comprehensive analytics."

[2:00 - 3:30] **Account Creation**
- Screen: Navigate to login page
- "Let's create your account. Click 'Create Account' and fill in your email, name, password, and organization name."
- Screen: Fill out registration form
- "Once registered, you'll be directed to the main dashboard."

[3:30 - 6:00] **Dashboard Tour**
- Screen: Navigate through sidebar items
- "The sidebar contains six main sections: Dashboard for your overview metrics, Campaigns for ad management, Inventory for supply management, Customers for your CDP data, Audiences for segment building, and Analytics for reporting."
- Demonstrate clicking each section with brief explanation

[6:00 - 7:30] **Key Concepts**
- "Before diving deeper, let's cover some key terms."
- "Impressions are the number of times your ad is displayed."
- "CTR is your click-through rate -- clicks divided by impressions."
- "CPM is cost per mille -- what you pay per thousand impressions."
- "Fill rate is the percentage of ad requests that return an ad."

[7:30 - 8:00] **Closing**
- "You are now ready to start using the platform. In our next video, we will create your first advertising campaign."

### V02: Creating Your First Campaign

**Duration**: 10 minutes
**Audience**: Advertisers
**Objective**: Walk through the complete campaign creation process.

**Script Outline:**

[0:00 - 0:30] **Introduction**
- "In this video, we will create a complete advertising campaign from scratch."

[0:30 - 2:00] **Campaign Setup**
- Screen: Navigate to Campaigns > New Campaign
- "Click 'New Campaign.' First, give your campaign a descriptive name."
- "Select your objective. 'Conversions' optimizes for conversion events. 'Awareness' maximizes impressions. 'Traffic' maximizes clicks."

[2:00 - 3:30] **Budget Configuration**
- "Set your total budget -- this is the maximum you will spend over the campaign lifetime."
- "Optionally set a daily budget to pace your spending evenly."
- "The platform's AI will automatically pace delivery to prevent early budget exhaustion."

[3:30 - 5:00] **Schedule & Line Items**
- "Set your start and end dates."
- "Now add a line item. Line items let you split your budget across different targeting strategies."
- "Set the CPM bid -- this is what you're willing to pay per thousand impressions."

[5:00 - 7:00] **Creative Upload**
- "Click 'Add Creative' and select your ad format."
- "Upload your creative file and enter the click-through URL."
- "Associate the creative with your line item."
- "You can add multiple creatives for A/B testing."

[7:00 - 8:30] **No-Code Builder Alternative**
- "For a simpler experience, try the No-Code Builder."
- Screen: Show drag-and-drop interface
- "Drag Goal, Audience, Creative, and Budget blocks to build your campaign visually."

[8:30 - 9:30] **Review & Submit**
- "Review your campaign summary. Check the budget, schedule, line items, and creatives."
- "Click 'Save as Draft' to save without activating, or 'Submit for Approval' to request activation."

[9:30 - 10:00] **Closing**
- "Your campaign is now ready. Once approved by an admin, it will begin serving ads in real-time auctions."

### V03: Publisher Inventory Setup

**Duration**: 8 minutes
**Audience**: Publishers
**Objective**: Guide publishers through inventory registration and management.

**Script Outline:**

[0:00 - 0:30] **Introduction**
- "In this video, we will set up your publisher profile and create your first inventory."

[0:30 - 2:00] **Publisher Registration**
- Screen: Navigate to Publishers > Add Publisher
- "Enter your publisher name and domain."
- "The revenue share determines your percentage of ad revenue. The default is 70%."

[2:00 - 3:30] **Adding Sites and Ad Units**
- "Add your website by entering the domain name."
- "Create ad units by specifying sizes and floor prices."
- "The floor price is the minimum CPM you will accept."

[3:30 - 5:30] **Creating Inventory**
- Screen: Navigate to Inventory > Create
- "Select your inventory type. Options include EMAIL for newsletter sponsorships, MOVIE for video ad placements, DISPLAY for banner ads, and NATIVE for in-content ads."
- Demonstrate creating an email inventory item
- Demonstrate creating a video inventory item

[5:30 - 7:00] **Monitoring Revenue**
- Screen: Navigate to Analytics
- "Your revenue dashboard shows total earnings, fill rate, and eCPM."
- "Use the yield optimization tool to get recommendations for adjusting floor prices."

[7:00 - 7:30] **Inventory Forecasting**
- "The forecasting tool predicts your available inventory for the next 30 days."

[7:30 - 8:00] **Closing**
- "Your inventory is now active and ready to monetize through our real-time auction system."

## 4. Production Notes

### Recording Requirements
- Screen resolution: 1920x1080
- Audio: Professional microphone, quiet environment
- Software: OBS Studio or Camtasia for screen recording
- Editing: Include captions/subtitles for accessibility
- Branding: Include platform logo and consistent color scheme

### Distribution
- Host on platform documentation site
- Embed in relevant training manual pages
- Upload to internal video library
- Provide downloadable versions for offline access
