# AdTech Platform Training Materials

## 1. Onboarding Modules

### Module 1: Platform Basics
- **Goal**: Understand the ecosystem (DSP, SSP, Ad Exchange).
- **Content**:
  - What is Real-Time Bidding (RTB)?
  - How does the AdTech Platform connect Advertisers and Publishers?
  - Terminology: CPM, CPC, CTR, Fill Rate.

### Module 2: For Advertisers - Maximizing ROI
- **Goal**: Create effective campaigns.
- **Content**:
  - Setting realistic budgets and bids.
  - Using targeting effectively (Geo, Device, Interest).
  - A/B testing creatives.

### Module 3: For Publishers - Maximizing Yield
- **Goal**: Optimize ad inventory.
- **Content**:
  - Placement strategies (Above the fold vs. Below).
  - Ad formats that pay best (Video vs. Banner).
  - Improving viewability.
